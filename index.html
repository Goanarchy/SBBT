100% OFFLINE & MAXIMUM PRIVACY GUARANTEED
This system operates 100% OFFLINE in your local browser. None of your confidential financials, quotes, client data, or numbers are transmitted to external servers. No internet connection required!

Personalize Your Company Profile

To generate invoices, estimates, work orders, and Excel sheets with your official business branding (Company Name, Address, Phone, NJ License #), click the blue 🏢 Company Profile button in the header or click below right now:

2
Create Projects & Record Sales (PROJECT is Mandatory)

In 💵 Invoicing & Sales, the PROJECT field is mandatory. The system automatically prefixes a date/document consecutive number (e.g. INV-2025-01 - Kitchen Remodel) and remembers it across the entire tool.

3
Assign Costs to Projects Across All Disciplines

When entering bills in 📦 Project COGS, Payroll, Fleet, Facility, or Utilities, select the project from the dropdown. Costs are automatically accumulated into that project's budget.

4
Audit Margins in "Project Cost Review"

Visit the 🏗️ Project Cost Review tab. Select any project to see all assigned cost lines, check/uncheck lines to simulate margins in real time, and view the progress bar vs contract price.

5
Save Consecutive Workbooks & Open Files

Click 💾 SAVE AS NEW FILE to generate a formatted Excel workbook with automatic consecutive naming (never overwriting old files). Use 📂 OPEN to load spreadsheets at any time.
