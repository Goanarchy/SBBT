⚙️ System Tools, Data Management & Settings

Easily backup your records, export executive reports, switch to a clean blank template, or request custom features without any risk of errors.

💾 Save Local Backup (JSON)

Downloads a complete snapshot of all your invoices, expenses, settings, and toggles directly to your computer. Keep this file safe as a personal backup or to transfer data between PCs.

📥 Load Backup File

Restores your data from an existing backup file on your PC. Automatically populates all 8 financial disciplines, keeping your real numbers intact without manual typing.

📊 Executive Excel Export (.xls)

Generates an executive-ready Excel workbook with live formulas, preformatted table headers, and dedicated tabs for every discipline. Perfect for your accountant, tax preparer, or bank loan officer.

🖨️ Print / Save to PDF

Formats the entire dashboard and financial statements into clean, print-ready pages. Use your browser's "Save as PDF" to create professional monthly PDF reports for your partners.

🔄 Restore Realistic Sample Data

Loads complete sample records for a New Jersey business (users, POs, Ford Transit fuel log, facility mortgage, payroll, utilities) so you can test features and view realistic charts.

🧹 Blank Slate (Clean Template for User)

Clears all demo records and resets the tool to an empty, clean state ($0.00 everywhere). Ideal for handing over to the user so they can enter their genuine business numbers from scratch.

How to Start — Quick 4-Step Guide

1
Pick Your Fiscal Period

At the top header, choose your active month (e.g. October) and year (2025). All dashboard numbers, graphs, and category tables immediately sync to this timeframe.

2
Record Quotes & User Invoices

Head to 💵 Invoicing & Sales. Enter quotes you have sent out (status "Quote / Sent") or active invoices. When the user pays, switch status to "Paid / Closed" to register cash.

3
Feed Your Expense Bills & Operational Costs

Click on 📦 Project COGS for job materials or subcontractor bills. Use the other tabs for Fleet, Facility, Insurance, Payroll, and Utilities. All dates are selected via dropdown with exact calendar days!

4
Monitor Your Executive Dashboard & Export to Excel

Return to 📈 Dashboard to see your live Inflow vs Outflow comparison, net profit in hand, and NJ tax reserves. When ready, click 📊 Excel Export to download a formatted spreadsheet for your CPA or bank.
